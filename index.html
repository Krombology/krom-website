Work by sector

Client names stay private. The numbers are real.

E-commerce, healthcare benefits

150 people moved from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365 over a weekend, with zero downtime.

Situation
A fast-growing online retailer in the healthcare space needed to standardise on Microsoft 365 ahead of a HiTrust audit. Years of Google Drive sharing and thousands of files with names Microsoft would reject.
Approach
Audited every Drive share and file name, fixed them by script, ran coexistence, migrated in batches, then compared source and destination file by file after the cut. Hardened Exchange Online Protection and Defender on the new side and trained the staff before Monday.
Outcome
Zero downtime. Sixty percent less manual migration effort through automation. Thirty-five percent SaaS cost reduction from licence decommissioning. HiTrust certification achieved with Krom as the infrastructure owner.

Environmental engineering

Three acquisitions folded into one Azure and Exchange Online estate.

Situation
A national engineering firm bought three companies over two years, each between 200 and 1,000 people, each with its own server room and on-prem Exchange.
Approach
On-site assessments of each acquired estate. Physical servers reprovisioned as Azure VMs with new VNets and NSGs. Mailboxes moved to Exchange Online with BitTitan and CloudM, MX cutovers scheduled around each firm's billing cycle.
Outcome
Legacy hardware decommissioned at all three sites. Acquired staff working on corporate identity and email from their first day on the new platform.

Environmental engineering

Over 100 offices re-networked and every file server upgraded, 30 percent faster afterwards.

Situation
Aging Cisco switching, flat networks, and Windows Server 2008 file servers across more than 100 locations.
Approach
VLAN segmentation, DNS, DHCP, and VPN rebuilt to a standard design. Windows Server 2008 to 2019 with DFS replication and namespaces, Dell EMC Unity storage. Runbooks written for the service desk.
Outcome
Network performance up 30 percent. Service desk resolution times down 25 percent after the knowledge base and escalation paths were built.

E-commerce, data platform

How an engagement runs

The same five steps whether it is a 50-seat migration or a three-company integration. You always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Assess

    One to two weeks. Inventory of identity, mail, files, servers, and devices. Who shares what with whom. What breaks if it moves.

  2. 2

    Plan

    A written integration path with scope, risk, and phasing per workstream. Communication templates for your people. A rollback for every cut.

  3. 3

    Execute

    Batches during business hours, cutovers at quiet ones. A status note every day the project is live.

  4. 4

    Stabilize

    Delta checks against the source. Fix what the migration tool missed. Tune spam filtering and security. Train the staff.

  5. 5

    Hand off

    Documentation and runbooks your team can run without me. Old licences cancelled. Or Krom stays on as your IT.
